# R1: first real-hardware bench (2026-11-24)

School laptop: ASUS BR1402CGA, Intel Core i3-N305, Intel UHD integrated graphics, 8 GB RAM, Windows, Prism Launcher,
1536 MiB max heap. Build: main `ce40bc7` (before package A). One run per scene per instance (vanilla = bench-only jar,
Sodium 0.9.2 + bench-only jar, Quicksilver), as in `docs/laptop-bench.md`.

## FPS

| scene | avg vanilla | avg Sodium | avg Quicksilver | 1% low vanilla | 1% low Sodium | 1% low Quicksilver |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| flythrough | 116 | 184 | 187 | 38 | 34 | 43 |
| spawn | 174 | 261 | 243 | 112 | 176 | 162 |
| water_glass | 244 | 373 | 393 | 159 | 204 | 209 |
| nether | 236 | 435 | 383 | 113 | 238 | 182 |
| mobs | 229 | 290 | 316 | 136 | 180 | 198 |
| spawn 600 s | 154 | – | 247 | 77 | – | 171 |

Quicksilver beats vanilla in every scene; the 30 FPS 1% low target is met everywhere (lowest 43, flythrough). Against
Sodium: ahead in flythrough, water_glass and mobs, behind in nether (average -12%, 1% low -24%) and spawn (average -7%,
1% low 162 vs 176).

## Other findings

- 0 fallbacks (sections Quicksilver's mesher handed back to vanilla's compiler).
- backFaces left out 34-44% of triangles.
- Heap after 10 minutes: Quicksilver 461 MB, vanilla 479 MB.
- Start-up to the title screen: Sodium 36.1 s, Quicksilver 37.2 s.
- Nether: Quicksilver p99 frame time 4.57 ms against Sodium's 3.59; 26 draws a frame against Sodium's 19; terrain 16.4
  draws for 145 section layers in 11 regions; occlusion culled 67% but logged "still frames drawn in full 81"; the mesher
  was still busy while measuring (2471 sections, 1.8 ms each).
- Spawn: terrain 41 draws for 677 section layers (Sodium 41 draws in total); occlusion culled only 21%.
- Draw calls in terrain-heavy scenes: 55-68 a frame against Sodium's 41-51.
- Flythrough: one frame uploaded 987 KB against an average budget of ~351 KB; one upload took 58 ms; 4 frames over 50 ms
  (max 70 ms), vanilla none.
- Server ticks over 50 ms in flythrough in all three (vanilla 12, Sodium 9, Quicksilver 8): worldgen, not the renderer.

Package B (real-hardware fixes) works through these; R2 is the next laptop bench.
