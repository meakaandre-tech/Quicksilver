# The core suite's border run (phase 2, P2-3b), played by the CI harness on the base world in every run alike (same
# format as scripted-run-core.txt; watch-chunks: ci/server/harness/.../ChunkWatch.java). Hoppers and containers on chunk
# borders, at the edge of a second force-loaded area that is let go at tick 300 (its chunks drop out of block ticking and
# out of full status), and hoppers taking from a chest minecart and a hopper minecart that cross a chunk border. It tests
# what logic.hopper-cache could change: a skipped chunk lookup that would have missed the server's last four chunks
# would give its chunk one tick less of a ticket. The chunk watch writes down every change to the watched chunks'
# tickets, levels and the last four chunks, tick by tick, and CI requires that history and the saved world (blocks,
# block entities, entities) equal to vanilla's (ci/server/compare-server.py, "Border run").
#
# Chunk levels before tick 300: area A (chunks -1..0) and area B (chunk 3) force-loaded (31); chunks 1, 2 and 4 next to
# them (32, block ticking); chunk 5 full (33). After: chunk 1 32, chunk 2 33 (full, not ticking), chunk 3 34, chunk 4 35.
# (Chunks this close to area A never unload from memory: that needs every ticket 11 chunks away, outside the base world.)
